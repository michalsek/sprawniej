# Welcome to Sprawniej

Sprawniej is where our team keeps track of work: tasks, bugs and ideas. This page explains everything you need.
No technical knowledge required.

## Getting in

A teammate sends you an **invite link**. Open it: it shows the workspace, who invited you and who's already there.
Press **Join workspace**, then:

1. **Create your account** with your email and a password (at least 8 characters), or press **Continue with
   GitHub** if you see it and have a GitHub account. The invite link lets you in, so you don't need a sign-up code.
   Already have an account? Choose **Sign in** instead.
2. **Pick your name and handle** ("What should we call you?"). Teammates see your name on issues and comments, and
   @mention you by your handle. You can change the handle later.

That's it: the workspace opens. An invite link works for as long as whoever made it chose. Some are for one person,
some for a few people or for anyone who has the link. If yours doesn't work any more, the page tells you why (see
**Something went wrong?** below): ask whoever invited you for a new one.

No invite link? Then you need a **sign-up code** to create an account: ask whoever looks after Sprawniej for one.
Open Sprawniej, choose **Create an account** and type the code in with your email and password (with GitHub, you type
it on the next screen, where you pick your name). Capital letters and dashes don't matter. A code works for a few days,
and for as many accounts as it was made for; if yours doesn't work any more, ask for a new one. Then pick your name,
and **Create a workspace**: give it a name and a first team. You're its owner.

The first time you open your team's issues, four short tips show you around. You can see them again from the
**Help** page (**Show the tour again**).

### Settings

Open **Settings** from the menu under your name (bottom left), or press `⌘K` and type what you're looking for
("password", "labels", "members"). In Settings, its own menu takes the sidebar's place:

- **Your account:** **Profile** (your name and @handle, light or dark, switching workspace, signing out) and **Ways
  to sign in**.
- **Workspace:** **General** (the workspace's name, leaving it), **Members**, **Invite links**, **People from Linear**
  (while some haven't joined), **Teams**, **Labels**, **Statuses**, **Archive**, **GitHub**, **Notion** and **Import from
  Linear**. Invite links and Import from Linear are only for owners and admins.

**Back to Sprawniej** at the top of the menu takes you back to where you were.

### More than one workspace

If you're in several workspaces, Sprawniej asks which one to open (the ones you opened lately come first). To go to
another one later, open the menu under your name (bottom left) and choose **Switch workspace**. The same list has
**Create a new workspace**.

### Your name

To go by another name, open **Settings** (in the menu under your name) and type it under **Your account ›
Profile**; it saves by itself. Changing your name doesn't change your handle.

### Your handle

Teammates @mention you by your handle. To change it, open **Settings** and type a new one under **Your account ›
Profile**, next to the @. It saves when you press Enter or click somewhere else; `Esc` puts the old one back. A handle
is letters, digits and dashes (up to 39), and it can't be one someone else uses now. Capitals don't matter for that,
and changing only the capitals of yours is fine.

It changes in every workspace you're in, and your teammates see the new one at once. Your issues, comments, filters
and history stay yours. What changes:
- **Old mentions turn into plain text.** Where someone wrote your old handle, it no longer mentions you: no
  highlight, and you don't hear about it. Nothing already written is changed.
- **You stop hearing about issues you were only mentioned in** (by your old handle). Issues you made, are assigned
  to, commented on or follow keep telling you what happens.
- **Someone else may take your old handle.** Then old text with that handle mentions them: it's highlighted as them,
  and they hear about those issues (new comments and status changes), just as if they had been mentioned there.
- Signing in doesn't change: your email, password and GitHub work as before.

### Light or dark

Sprawniej comes in a light look and a dark one. At first it follows your device: light by day, dark at night, if your
device switches. To pick one yourself, open **Settings › Profile** and choose under **Appearance**: **Light** or
**Dark** keep that look whatever your device does, and **System** goes back to following it. It's quicker from
anywhere: press `⌘` `K` (or `Ctrl` `K`) and type **appearance** (or **light** / **dark**) to find **Use light
appearance**, **Use dark appearance** and **Follow your device's appearance**; the one in use has a check. Your choice
saves by itself and follows you to every device you sign in on. Only you see it.

### Ways to sign in

**Settings › Ways to sign in** lists how you can sign in:

- **Email and password.** **Change password** picks a new one. Signed up with GitHub? **Set a password** lets you
  sign in with your email too.
- **GitHub.** **Add GitHub** takes you to GitHub and back; from then on **Continue with GitHub** signs you in.
  **Remove** stops it, when you added GitHub to an account made with your email. An account made with GitHub keeps
  GitHub, even after you set a password. If it says signing in with GitHub isn't switched on yet, use your email and
  password. Adding GitHub also lets your work on GitHub move your issues (see GitHub, below).

**Forgot your password?** Sprawniej can't email you a new one yet. If you also added GitHub, sign in with GitHub and
choose **Change password**. Otherwise, ask whoever looks after Sprawniej for help.

### Inviting someone

Only owners and admins can invite. Open the menu under your name (bottom left), choose **Settings**, then **Invite
links** (or **Invite people** on **Members**), and press **Create an invite link**. Then choose:

- **Who can use it**: **One person** (the usual choice), **Several people** (up to a number you pick, from 1 to
  100), or **Anyone** who has the link. A link for anyone lets everyone who sees it create an account and join, so
  share it only where the right people will see it, for example in your team's own chat.
- **How long** it works: **1 week**, **2 weeks** (the usual choice) or **Custom**, from 1 to 28 days. Under the
  choice you see the day and time it stops working.
- Their **email** (optional, only for one person), so you remember where to send it, and whether they join as a
  **Member** or an **Admin**.

Press **Create link**, then **Copy message** gives you a ready-to-send note with the link, **Copy link** just the
link, and **Write an email** (when you added their email) opens your own mail app. Sprawniej doesn't send anything
itself: you send the link.

**Invite links** lists every link that still works, and the ones that stopped. Each shows who it's for, the role, who
made it and when it stops working. When people have joined with it, it says how many (**3 joined**): press that to see
who, and on which day. A link for one person leaves the list once it's used; that person shows up under **Members**.

To stop a link from working (for example, if you sent it to the wrong person, or the team has all joined), open the
**⋯** next to it and choose **End link now**: nobody new can join with it, and the list still shows who joined.
Ending can't be undone, but you can always make a new link. A link that ended, expired or is full stays in the list,
greyed out, until you press **Delete link**. Deleting a link also forgets who joined with it (they stay in the
workspace). **Copy message** is in the same menu.

### Members and roles

**Settings › Members** lists everyone in the workspace. Everyone can see and change every issue, project, view and
label, and make teams. On top of that:

- **Owners** can do everything, including making or changing other owners.
- **Admins** invite people, change roles (except owners'), remove people (except owners), rename the workspace,
  delete teams and import from Linear.
- **Members** work on issues, projects and views.

Owners and admins change someone's role with the menu next to their name, and remove someone with **⋯ › Remove from
workspace**. Removed people can't open the workspace any more; their issues and comments stay, with their name. To
bring them back, send them a new invite. Links made before someone left or was removed don't bring them back, so
you don't need to end those links.

### Leaving a workspace

To leave a workspace yourself, press **Leave workspace** in **Settings › General**. Your issues and comments
stay, with your name; to come back, ask for a new invite. Your favourites there come back with you, except anything
deleted while you were away. A workspace always keeps at least one owner: if you're the only one, make someone else
an owner first.

## Your work saves by itself

There is no Save button. Whatever you type or change is kept right away. In the top bar you'll see:

- **Saved**: everything is safe and your teammates can see it.
- **Saving…**: it's on its way, give it a second.
- **Offline**: no internet right now. Keep working and keep the tab open; your changes are saved when you're back
  online. If you try to close the tab before that, Sprawniej asks first.
- **Not saved yet**: Sprawniej can't be reached for a moment. It keeps trying; wait until it says Saved before you
  close the tab.
- **Reconnecting…**: your work is saved, and your teammates' changes will show up again in a moment.
- **Signed out**: sign in again to keep working. Everything on screen stays as it is.

Sprawniej needs the internet to save. If you sign out or switch workspace while something isn't saved yet, it asks
first.

If someone else's change and yours can't both be kept (say, they deleted an issue you were editing), a short message
says what wasn't saved.

Your teammates' changes show up on their own, as soon as they're saved.

### New versions

When a new version of Sprawniej is out, a small note says so, and **Update available** appears in the top bar next
to **Saved**. Nothing changes until you choose: press it when it suits you (whatever isn't saved yet is saved first,
then the page reloads), or just carry on, and the new version loads the next time you open Sprawniej.

To see what changed, open the menu under your name and choose **What's new**: every version, newest first.

## Issues

An **issue** is one piece of work: a task, a bug, an idea. Each has a short ID like `ENG-12` (team + number).

- **Create one:** press **C** anywhere, the pencil next to "Sprawniej" at the top left, or the **+** next to a group.
  Give it a title; status, priority, assignee, labels, project, parent, due date and estimate are optional buttons
  below.
  Press `⌘` `Enter` (or `Ctrl` `Enter`) to create it. Tick **Create more** to add several, one after another. Closed
  it by accident? What you typed comes back the next time you start a new issue.
- **Open one:** click it. Change the title, description or any property on the right. It saves by itself.
- **Labels:** in the labels menu, type a name that doesn't exist yet and choose **Create label "…"** (with the name
  you typed).
- **Move to another team:** change **Team** on the right. The issue gets that team's next number; links and mentions
  of its old number still open it.
- **Archive:** the `⋯` button next to the title, then **Archive** (see Archive below).
- **Delete:** the `⋯` button next to the title, then **Delete issue**.
- **Links and formatting:** paste a link and it becomes clickable. Select some words and paste a link to turn
  them into a link. Type `/` at the start of a line (or after a space) for a list of what you can add: a checklist,
  a heading, a bulleted or numbered list, a quote, a code block, a table or a divider. Keep typing to narrow it down
  (`/tab` finds Table, `/heading 2` finds Heading 2), pick with the arrows and `Enter` or a tap, and `Esc` closes it.
  Or type it straight away and it formats as you type: `# `, `## ` or `### ` for a heading, `- ` for a list, `1. `
  for a numbered list, `[] ` for a checklist, `> ` for a quote, ```` ``` ```` for code, `---` for a divider,
  `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `~~struck~~` and `` `code` ``.
- **Tables:** pick **Table** from the `/` list. `Tab` goes to the next cell, and `Tab` in the last cell adds a line.
  Inside a table, `/` adds a line below or a column to the right, and removes a line, a column or the whole table.
  A cell holds a little text (`Shift` `Enter` starts a new line in it). On a phone, a wide table scrolls sideways.
- **Mention someone:** type `@` and their name or handle. They'll get a note in their Inbox. A mention counts by the
  handle someone has now (see Your handle).
- **Point to another issue:** type its ID, like `ENG-7`. Hold `⌘` (or `Ctrl`) and click it to open it.

### Checklists

Break an issue into steps and tick them off as you go.

- **Start one:** type `[] ` at the start of a line (also in a bulleted list), or pick **Checklist** from the `/`
  list. `Enter` adds the next item, and `Enter` on an empty item ends the checklist. `Tab` puts an item under the one
  above it; `Shift` `Tab` takes it back out.
- **Tick an item:** click or tap its box. You don't need to be writing in the description, and on a phone the
  keyboard stays away. With the cursor in an item, `⌘` `Shift` `Enter` (or `Ctrl` `Shift` `Enter`) ticks it too.
- **See how far along it is:** lists and boards show a checklist mark with a count like `2/5` (two of five items
  ticked) on issues that have a checklist.
- **Ticking at the same time:** when you and a teammate tick items at once, both ticks stay, even on items right next
  to each other. A tick made while you're offline is saved once you're back, without undoing anything a teammate
  changed meanwhile.
- **"That checklist item changed meanwhile":** a teammate reworded or removed the item just as you ticked it. Your
  tick is taken back; have a look at the new text and tick it again.
- **In comments:** checklists work there too. The person who wrote a comment can tick its boxes without opening it
  for editing; nobody else can (tapping a box you can't tick says why).

### Working at the same time as others

You never have to choose between your changes and a teammate's. If you both change different things, both stay. If
you both change the same thing (say, the status), the one saved last stays. If you both type in the same description
at once, their words come into yours as you type; if you both finish at the very same moment, the text saved last
stays. Ticking a checklist item saves just that box, so two people ticking at once both keep their ticks. Two people
adding issues at once always get different numbers.

### Statuses

Every issue has a status, and every status belongs to one of seven **groups**. A workspace starts with one status in
each, named like its group:

- **Backlog**: we might do it some day.
- **Todo**: we plan to do it soon.
- **In Progress**: someone is working on it.
- **In Review**: it's done and waiting for someone to check it.
- **Done**: finished.
- **Canceled**: we decided not to do it.
- **Duplicate**: someone already reported the same thing.

The group decides what a status means: which tab shows the issue (Backlog, or Active for Todo, In Progress and In
Review), and whether it counts as finished (Done, Canceled and Duplicate are archived after a while). Within a group,
your workspace can have as many statuses as it likes, say **Testing** and **Design review** in In Progress, or
**Released** in Done. Pick one in the status menu (or press `S`), drag a card into its column, or filter by it.

**Settings › Statuses** is where anyone in the workspace adds, renames, recolours, reorders and hides statuses,
group by group. Everything saves as you go:

- **Add status** at the end of a group makes a new one, ready to name. Two statuses can't have the same name.
- The status's icon opens the colours; the name changes in place.
- Drag the handle (⠿) to change the order, or focus it and press `Space`, the arrow keys, then `Space` again. On a
  phone, press and hold the handle, or use **⋯ › Move up** and **Move down**. The order is the order of menus,
  filters and board columns.
- The first status of a group that isn't hidden is where an issue lands when only its group is known: GitHub, the
  Linear import, and teammates on an older version of Sprawniej put issues there. When a group has more than one,
  that one is marked **Default**.
- On a computer, hover **Hidden** or **Default** to read what they mean.
- **Hide** (on a phone, the eye) takes a status out of the menus. Issues that have it keep it and still show it,
  and so do their history and the filters; **Show** brings it back. Statuses are never deleted. Each group keeps at
  least one status that isn't hidden, so the last one can't be hidden.
- Next to each status: how many issues have it now.

### Priority

**Urgent** (orange), **High**, **Medium**, **Low** (fewer bars = lower), or no priority (three dashes).

### Due dates and estimates

- **Due date:** the day it should be done. Pick **Due date** on the right: **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **Next week**,
  **In two weeks**, or any day in the calendar. Lists and boards show it; it turns amber two days before and red
  once it's late (unless the issue is finished).
- **Estimate:** how big it is, in points: 1, 2, 3, 5 or 8. Bigger means more work. Pick **Estimate** on the right,
  or press `E` on an issue.

To see what's due first, choose **Ordering › Due date** under **Display**.

### Sub-issues

Big issues can be split into smaller **sub-issues**. The parent shows a counter like `2/5` (two of five done).

- **Add some:** open the issue and choose **Add sub-issues** under the description (or **+** next to "Sub-issues").
  Type a title and press `Enter`; the field stays open for the next one. `Esc` closes it. **More options** opens
  the full new issue form.
- **Make an existing issue a sub-issue:** change **Parent** on the right.

### Comments and history

Under each issue, **Activity** shows its comments and everything that happened to it, oldest first: who changed the
status, who it was given to, when the due date moved, and so on. It shows every change made in Sprawniej, and your
teammates' changes appear as they make them (it needs a connection to show). Changes from before your work was
brought over aren't shown. Issues that were finished and left alone for a while are archived by themselves at
night; their history says "Archived automatically".

Talk about an issue in the comments. Type, then press `⌘` `Enter` or **Comment**. Formatting, links,
`@` mentions and issue IDs work just like in descriptions. A comment you haven't sent yet waits in this browser,
so you can leave and come back.

To change or remove one of your own comments, use the `⋯` on it: **Edit** (it saves as you type; **Done** when
you're finished) or **Delete**. Only the person who wrote a comment can change or delete it.

### Subscribing

You hear about comments and status changes on issues you **follow**. You follow an issue by itself when you created
it, it's assigned to you, you commented on it, or someone mentioned you in it. To follow any other issue, press the
bell next to its title; press it again to stop (even for your own issues). **Subscribers** on the right shows who
follows it, and you can add or remove teammates there too.

## Lists and boards

Every list of issues can be shown two ways. Switch with **List / Board** at the top right.

- **List:** issues grouped by status, in the order of Settings › Statuses. Sub-issues sit just under their parent.
- **Board:** a column per status, with cards. A hidden status gets a column only while an issue in view has it.
  Empty columns wait under **Hidden columns** on the right, together with statuses the tab leaves out (like **Done**
  on the Active tab). Drop a card on one to move the issue there; the column then shows up with that card in it.

**Drag and drop** works in both. Drag an issue up or down to change its order, or into another group or column to
change its status. Your teammates see the new order too. No mouse? Move to an issue with `Tab` (or `J` and `K`),
press `Space` to pick it up, use the arrow keys, and press `Space` again to drop it (`Esc` puts it back). On a phone,
press and hold an issue for a moment, then drag it.

**Several at once:** hold `⌘` (or `Ctrl`) and click issues to pick them, or press `X` on each. `Shift`-click picks
everything between the last one you picked and this one. Drag any picked issue and the others come along, side by
side. The bar at the bottom changes the status, priority, assignee, labels or estimate of all of them (or archives
or deletes them); `S`, `P`, `A`, `L` and `E` do the same. `Esc` lets go of them.

**Display** (next to List / Board) changes how the page looks, just for you:

- **Grouping:** by status, assignee, priority, project, or **No grouping**. Dropping an issue into a group gives it
  that group's assignee, priority or project.
- **Ordering:** **Manual** is the order you and your teammates arrange by dragging. You can also sort by priority,
  by due date, by last update, or newest first. Dragging to reorder only works in Manual order.
- **Show sub-issues** hides or shows them. **Show finished issues** does the same for finished ones, on pages that
  can have them (not on the Active or Backlog tab, which never show finished issues).
- **Back to default** undoes your changes. It shows up once you've changed something (a saved view has none: its
  display is everyone's).

On your team's page, the tabs show:

- **Active**: Todo, In Progress and In Review.
- **Backlog**: ideas for later.
- **All issues**: everything, including finished work.
- **Archived**: issues put away (see below), with a search box.

## Archive

Finished issues leave the lists by themselves some time after they're done (six months unless your team picks
otherwise in **Settings › Archive**). That keeps lists short and the app quick. You can also archive any issue yourself
from its `⋯` menu, or several at once from the bar at the bottom; an issue's sub-issues go with it.

Archived issues aren't gone. Find them under the **Archived** tab of their team, or with `⌘` `K` search; links to them
still work. An archived issue can be read but not changed. Press **Restore** on it to bring it back, with its
comments, under the same ID.

## Teams

Your teams are in the sidebar under **Your teams**. Each team has its own **Issues**, **Projects** and **Views**.
Press **+** next to *Your teams* to join a team or create a new one. If you're in no team yet, Sprawniej asks which
teams you work with when you open the workspace; **Skip for now** if you'd rather look around first.
**Settings › Teams** lists every team in the workspace: **Join** one you're not in, or use **⋯** to edit, leave or
delete yours, and **Create a team** makes a new one.

Point at a team in the sidebar and press **⋯** to:

- **Edit team:** change its emoji and name. It saves as you go. The short key (`ENG`) stays, so issue links keep working.
- **Leave team:** it leaves your sidebar; the team and its issues stay for everyone else. Join again with **+**.
- **Delete team** (admins and owners only): removes the team for everyone, with its issues, comments, archive and its
  own views. Projects stay, without that team. You'll be asked to type the team's name first, because this can't be
  undone.

## Filters

**Filter** (above every list) narrows it down: by status, assignee, subscribers, priority, labels, project or team.
A page leaves out the filter it already is: there's no Team filter on a team's own page (or a team's view), no
Assignee filter on My issues and no Project filter on a project's page.
**Subscribers** finds the issues someone follows: ones they made, are assigned to, commented on, were mentioned in or
subscribed to. Each filter shows as a chip. Click a chip to change it, or its **×** to remove it. Inside one filter,
any of the chosen values counts ("Todo or In Progress"); several filters must all match ("Todo, and assigned to Ana").
In the Status filter, ticking every status of a group keeps the whole group, so a status added to it later is in too;
ticking only some keeps just those.

Filters you set on a team's page or on My issues are just for you, and go away when you leave the page. To keep
them, press **Save as view**.

## Views

A **view** is a saved set of filters with its own emoji, like "🐞 Open bugs" or "📊 Insights". Open **Views** in the
sidebar to see them, and **New view** to make one. A view shows issues from the whole workspace or from one team.

A view belongs to everyone. Changing its name, emoji, description, filters, grouping or List / Board saves straight
away, for everyone. In the list of views, click a view's emoji to change it, or use **⋯** to rename or delete it. On
the view's own page, click its name or description to change them. Deleting a view leaves its issues as they are.
Press the star (☆) on a view to keep it in your sidebar (see Favourites).

## Projects

A **project** is a bigger goal made of several issues, like "New onboarding". **New project** on the Projects page
starts one; a new project starts in Planned. On its page you can change its emoji, name and description in place, and
set its status, who leads it, a target date and which teams work on it. The bar shows how many of its issues are done.

The Projects page (for the whole workspace, and for each team) shows projects as a **List** or a **Board**; switch
with the buttons next to New project. Sprawniej remembers your choice for each page, in this browser.

- **List**: every project by name, with its status, lead, target date and progress. Click an emoji to change it, or
  use **⋯** to rename or delete a project.
- **Board**: a column for each project status (Backlog, Planned, In progress, Paused, Completed, Canceled, and any
  your workspace adds), with how many projects are in it. Drag a card to another column to change its status, or up
  and down to put it in order; the order is the same for everyone. **+** at the top of a column starts a project in
  that status. On a phone, press and hold a card to pick it up. With the keyboard, move to a card, press Space to pick
  it up, the arrow keys to move it, Space to drop it and Esc to put it back.
- A hidden status keeps its column, marked **Hidden**, while projects are in it. You can drag projects out of it, but
  not into it.

Add issues to a project from the project's page, or pick the project on any issue. Deleting a project (**⋯ › Delete
project**) keeps its issues; they just no longer belong to a project. Press the star (☆) on a project to keep it in your
sidebar (see Favourites).

## Favourites

Keep the projects and views you open often in your sidebar: they show in the **Workspace** section, under
**Projects** and **Views**.

- **Add one:** press the star (☆) next to **⋯** at the top of a project's or view's page, or next to its name in the
  list of projects or views (it shows when you point at it). Or open ⌘K on its page and pick **Add to favourites**. A
  new favourite goes to the bottom.
- **Open one:** click it in the sidebar.
- **Change the order:** drag it up or down. With the keyboard, move to it with `Tab`, press `Space` to pick it up, use
  the arrow keys, and press `Space` again to put it down (`Esc` puts it back). Or use its **⋯ › Move up** or
  **Move down**.
- **Remove one:** press the star again, use **⋯ › Remove from favourites** in the sidebar, or **Remove from
  favourites** in ⌘K.

Your favourites are only yours: teammates don't see them, and yours don't change theirs. Each workspace has its own.
When a project or view is deleted, it leaves your favourites by itself. Finished projects stay until you remove them.

## Labels

Labels like "Bug" or "Design" sort issues across teams. Make one right from an issue (type a new name in the
labels menu), or in **Settings › Labels**, where you can also rename, recolour and delete them. Changes apply to
every issue that has the label. Many labels come in pages of 10, with **Search labels** above them.

## Inbox

Your **Inbox** tells you when someone:

- assigns you an issue,
- mentions you with `@` in a description or a comment,
- comments on an issue you follow (see Subscribing above),
- changes the status of an issue you follow.

The number in the sidebar is how many you haven't read. Opening a note (or its issue) marks it read. Hover a note to
mark it read or delete it; **Mark all as read** and the `⋯` menu at the top work on all of them. Read notes clear
themselves after a month, and unread ones after three.

## GitHub

When your team connects a GitHub repository, its pull requests move the issues they mention:

- a pull request opens, or a draft becomes ready for review: the issue moves to **In Review**;
- it's merged: the issue moves to **Done**.

If your workspace has more than one status in a group, the issue moves to the group's first one that isn't hidden
(marked **Default** in Settings › Statuses). An issue already in any In Review status isn't moved to review again.

Mention an issue by its number, like `ENG-12`, in the branch name (`eng-12-fix-login` works too), the pull request's
title or its description. One pull request can move several issues. An issue that moved to another team is found by
its old number as well. Nothing moves backwards: an issue that is already Done, Canceled or a Duplicate stays as it
is, and so does an archived one. A pull request closed without merging, or still a draft, moves nothing.

The issue also lists its pull requests under its title, each with how it stands: **Open**, **Draft**, **Merged** or
**Closed**. Click one to open it on GitHub. The list changes as soon as something happens on GitHub, including for
drafts and pull requests closed without merging, which don't move the issue. Taking the issue's number out of a pull
request's title or description takes it off the list.

The change is made as the person who opened the pull request, so the issue's history says who and links to the pull
request ("Ana moved it from In Progress to In Review · pull request #12"), and everyone following the issue gets a
note in their Inbox. This only works for **people in the workspace who added GitHub as a way to sign in**
(Settings › Ways to sign in): pull requests by anyone else move nothing and don't show on the issue. **Settings ›
GitHub** tells you whether yours count.

**Connecting a repository** (owners and admins): **Settings › GitHub › Connect a repository**, then type it as
`owner/name` or paste its address. The repository also needs the Sprawniej GitHub App; whoever set up Sprawniej adds
it. Each repository shows what its latest pull request did, so you can check it works. **Remove** stops its pull
requests moving issues; issues that already moved stay where they are.

## Notion

If your team keeps a database of tasks in Notion, a page there can become an issue here with one press. It needs a
paid Notion plan (Plus, Business or Enterprise): the free plan has no "Send webhook".

**Setting it up** (owners and admins): open **Settings**, then **Notion** under Workspace, and press **Connect
Notion**. Pick the team new issues go to and press **Make the address**, then **Copy**. The address is shown only
once, so paste it straight into Notion: in your Notion database, add a **database button** (or an **automation**, for
example "when Status is set to Ready") with the action **Send webhook**, and paste the address as its URL. In an
automation, tick the properties to send. Members see who can connect it instead.

Once it's connected, **Settings › Notion** shows the team pages go to (pick another and it changes at once), what
the last page did ("Last page: “Fix login” became ENG-12") and when the address was made.

**What comes over.** Notion sends a page's properties, not its text. The issue gets the page's title (a page without
one is "Untitled") and a link back to the page, plus whatever of these the page has:

- **Status**: Not started, To do or Ready become Todo; In progress or Doing become In Progress; In review or QA
  become In Review; Done, Complete or Shipped become Done; Canceled or Won't do become Canceled; Backlog or Later
  become Backlog. Anything else is Todo.
- **Priority**: Urgent (or P0), High (P1), Medium (P2), Low (P3). Anything else is No priority.
- **Assignee** (or Assignees, Assigned to, Owner): the first person who is in this workspace with the same email.
- **Due** (or Due date, Deadline): the due date.
- **Labels** (or Tags): the labels with the same names that the workspace already has. New ones aren't made.
- **Team**: a team's key or name (ENG or Engineering). Otherwise the team picked in Settings.
- **Description** (or Details, Notes): the description, with its bold, italics, code and links.
- **Created by**: who made the issue, when they're in this workspace with the same email.

Names are matched without caring about capitals or spaces, so "due date" works as well as "Due date".

**Who made it.** Add a **Created by** property to the Notion database: when the person who made the page is in this
workspace with the same email, the issue is theirs ("Ana created the issue from Notion"). Otherwise it says
"Created from Notion". The assignee and anyone @mentioned in the description get a note in their Inbox, shown as
from Notion when nobody here made the issue.

**Sending a page again** keeps the one issue it made, and changes nothing in it, so pressing the button twice is fine.
After its issue is deleted, the page makes a new one. Pages in Notion's trash are skipped.

**When Notion shows "!"** next to the button or automation, Notion paused it because Sprawniej said no.
**Settings › Notion** shows what the last page did:

- "Last page wasn't added: pick a team", or "The team for Notion pages was deleted": pick another team, then resume
  it in Notion.
- "Pages were coming too fast": more than 100 pages in 10 minutes. Wait a few minutes, then resume it.
- Neither of those? Then Notion has an old address: it was replaced, or Notion was disconnected. Settings › Notion
  doesn't hear about pages sent to an old address, so it stays as it was. Paste the current address into Notion
  (connect again first if it was disconnected), then resume it.

**Replace address** makes a new address and stops the old one at once (if you think someone else has it). Copy it
and paste it into Notion in place of the old one. **Disconnect** stops pages becoming issues; issues already made
stay.

## Moving over from Linear

**Settings › Import from Linear** brings your teams, issues, comments, projects, labels and views over. Only admins and
owners can import. You'll need a Linear personal API key (the first step shows where to make one). Pick the teams,
check who's who, and press **Import**. Issue numbers stay the same (`ENG-123` is still `ENG-123`), and so do due
dates and estimates. Issues finished long ago go straight to the archive. Views keep the filters that fit, and the
import tells you which views lost some, so you can check them. If the import stops partway (say, the connection
dropped), what came over so far stays: press **Try again** to bring over the rest.

People who aren't in Sprawniej yet come over too, listed under **Settings › People from Linear**: their issues,
comments and views stay theirs. Owners and admins have two ways to hand them their work:

- **Invite**: makes an invite link just for that person (with their email from Linear, if known). Such a link is
  always for one person; you only choose how long it works. When they join with it, they become that person:
  everything that was theirs in Linear is theirs.
- **Link**: for someone who already joined under another name. Pick who they are here, and everything moves to them.
  Linking can't be undone, so check the name first.

You can run the import again later to bring in new changes; nothing is copied twice, and people you linked stay
linked. Picking someone who has joined for a person from Linear on the import's **Who's who?** step links them too.

Searching anywhere (menus, @mentions, ⌘K, the archive) doesn't need Polish letters: "lukasz" finds Łukasz.

## Handy keys

- `⌘` `K` (or `Ctrl` `K`), or `/`: search issues and jump anywhere.
- `C`: new issue.
- `⌘` `K`, then **appearance** (or **light** / **dark**): switch between the light and the dark look.
- `S`: change the status of the issue under the mouse (or the open one).
- `P`: change the priority.
- `A`: change who it's assigned to.
- `L`: change the labels.
- `E`: change the estimate.
- `J` / `K`, or `↓` / `↑`: move to the next or previous issue in a list or board.
- `X`: pick the issue (to change or move several at once).
- `Enter`: open it.
- `Space`: pick it up to move it with the arrows; `Space` again puts it down. The same works for a favourite in the
  sidebar.
- `Esc`: let go of picked issues, or go back from an issue.

While writing a description or a comment:

- `/`: the list of what you can add (headings, lists, a checklist, a quote, code, a table, a divider).
- `⌘` `B` bold, `⌘` `I` italic, `⌘` `Shift` `S` struck, `⌘` `E` code (with `Ctrl` instead of `⌘` on Windows).
- `⌘` `Alt` `1`, `2` or `3`: a heading. `⌘` `Alt` `C`: a code block. `⌘` `Shift` `B`: a quote.
- `⌘` `Shift` `8` a bulleted list, `⌘` `Shift` `7` a numbered list, `⌘` `Shift` `9` a checklist.
- `⌘` `Shift` `Enter`: tick the checklist item you're in.
- `⌘` `Enter`: send a comment.

## On a phone

Sprawniej works in your phone's browser too. The menu button at the top left opens the sidebar; in Settings, the
same button opens the settings menu. Press and hold an issue to drag it, or a favourite in the sidebar to change their
order. In the lists of projects and views, the star always shows on a phone. Tap a checklist box to tick it (the
keyboard stays away). A wide table scrolls sideways.

## Pictures

Paste a picture (or drop one) into a description or a comment. It's kept with your workspace, so only your
teammates can see it. PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP pictures can be added, up to 10 MB each. The same picture pasted
twice is kept once. A grey space shows where a picture goes until it has loaded.

## Something went wrong?

- **An invite link that doesn't work**: the page says why, and the answer is always to ask whoever invited you for a
  new link.
  - **"This invite link doesn't work"**: it was deleted, or part of the link is missing.
  - **"This invite was already used"**: it was for one person, who has joined.
  - **"This invite link was ended"**: whoever made it ended it early.
  - **"This invite link is full"**: as many people as it allows have joined with it.
  - **"This invite has expired"**: its time is over.
  - **"This invite link has no places left"**: people who are still creating their accounts hold its places. If you
    already have an account, press **Sign in instead**.
  - **"This link was made before you left"**: you left the workspace, or were removed, after it was made.
  - **"This invite link is from an older Sprawniej"**: it's from before invite links worked the way they do now.
- **"You're no longer in this workspace"** or **"This workspace isn't here any more"**: someone removed you, or the
  workspace was deleted. If that's a mistake, ask an owner or an admin for a new invite.
- **"Not saved yet" for a long time**: check your internet connection and keep the tab open. Sprawniej keeps trying
  and says **Saved** once it's done.
- **"Please sign in again"** or **Signed out** in the top bar: you were signed out, maybe because it's been a while.
  Sign in, and you carry on where you were.
- **"We couldn't open this workspace"**: check your connection and press **Try again**.
- **"Sprawniej isn't set up yet"**: this copy of Sprawniej isn't ready. Tell whoever looks after Sprawniej.
- **Using a shared computer?** Open the menu under your name at the bottom left, choose **Sign out…**, and press
  **Sign out** on the page that opens. It signs you out in that browser (in every tab) and removes the copy of your
  workspaces it kept there.
